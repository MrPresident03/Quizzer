Quizzer is trivia that keeps you learning — and keeps the questions fresh.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Everyone gets the same 10 questions each day, from easy to hard. Play once, then share your score as a spoiler-free 🟩🟥 grid and see how your friends did.

QUESTIONS THAT DON'T REPEAT
Quizzer remembers what you've already seen and serves new questions first. Hundreds of hand-checked questions are built in and work offline, and thousands more are added from the Open Trivia Database when you're online.

LEARN FROM EVERY ANSWER
See why each answer is right the moment you answer. Every question you miss goes into Review Mistakes, and comes back until you get it right.

20 TOPICS, PLUS FLAGS AND CAPITALS
History, science, geography, sport, film and music, literature, art, food, technology, maths, mythology, animals, law, business, language and more. Test yourself on the flags of 185 countries and the capitals of nearly 100.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Quick Play: one tap starts a quiz with your favourite settings
• Mix any topics, pick a difficulty, choose 10, 20, 30 or Endless
• Multiple choice or true/false, timed (20 seconds) or relaxed
• Free lifelines every quiz: 50/50, skip, extra time and ask the audience

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Daily streaks with earned streak freezes, XP and levels, 20 badges, an activity graph and accuracy for every topic — with a one-tap drill for your weakest one.

MADE TO BE PLEASANT
No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases. Four colour themes, dark mode, larger text, sound and haptics, and an optional evening reminder for the Daily Challenge.
