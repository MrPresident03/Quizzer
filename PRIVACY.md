# Quizzer privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Quizzer ("the app") is a trivia game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **the app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.**

## What stays on your device

Your profile name and avatar, quiz history, scores, XP, streak, badges, settings and the list of questions you've seen or missed are stored **only on your device**. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Uninstalling the app, or using **Settings → Reset All Profile Progress**, deletes this data.

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by your Google account, not by the app's developer.

## Internet access

When you're online, the app downloads trivia questions from the [Open Trivia Database](https://opentdb.com) (opentdb.com). These requests contain only quiz settings (such as topic and difficulty) and an anonymous session token issued by that service so it doesn't repeat questions. No personal information is sent. As with any website request, opentdb.com can see your device's IP address; its own policies apply to that service.

The app works offline using its built-in questions.

## Notifications

If you turn on the optional **Daily Reminder**, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No push service or server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

## Sharing

If you tap **Share** on a Daily Challenge result, the app opens Android's share sheet with a short text summary (your score and a grid of coloured squares). Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share it with.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
- No accounts, contacts, location, camera, microphone or files access
- No in-app purchases

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
